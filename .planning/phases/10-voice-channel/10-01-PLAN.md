The voice channel architecture:
- Home Assistant Assist receives audio from ESPHome satellites or iPhone HA Companion
- Whisper (Wyoming protocol) STTs the audio to text
- HA sends the transcribed text as a POST to Nova's `/v1/chat/completions` (the "HA proxy endpoint")
- Nova runs the agent loop and returns a text response
- Piper (Wyoming protocol) TTS speaks the response aloud in the satellite/iPhone

The "HA proxy endpoint" IS the existing `/v1/chat/completions` — there is no separate voice endpoint. Tests validate this contract.
</context>
